Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

AI Channel Uploader is used only by its owner, Rahul Mourya, to upload videos to YouTube channels he owns.

Data accessed

The app requests permission to upload videos and set thumbnails on the owner's YouTube channels (YouTube Data API). It does not read, collect, sell or share any personal data of other people.

Storage

The authorization token is stored only as an encrypted secret in the owner's private automation settings and is used solely to upload the owner's own videos.

Third parties

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
